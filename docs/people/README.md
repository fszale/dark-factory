## Rules for this folder

- Quote exactly and link the source. Paraphrase is labeled as paraphrase.
- Separate what a person has shipped from what they have announced and from what we infer.
- Times are US Eastern (ET) unless a source timestamp is quoted as is.
- Append to the log. Do not rewrite older entries; correct them with a new dated entry.
- Outreach notes are drafts for Filip. Nothing in this folder is sent or posted by an agent.
